About Surat BRTS

Surat's leading eco-friendly bus rapid transit initiative.

Our Mission

To deliver rapid, safe, carbon-conscious, and technologically modern public mobility across the diamond city of Surat.

Vision & Expansion

Developing an integrated transport network linking BRTS Corridors, Metro routes, and electric City buses for smart city development.

Environmental Sustainability Impact

Surat BRTS operates over 80+ zero-emission electric buses. Our corridors save approximately 500+ Metric Tons of CO2 emissions monthly by diverting passengers from private vehicles to public transit. Our goal is 100% electrification of the transit fleet by 2028.
